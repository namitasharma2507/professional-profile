# Academic Projects

## 1. EarGuard

**Tech Stack:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript

A web-based project focused on promoting safe earbud usage by applying the ISO 1999 sound-dose concept to represent hearing-risk levels and create awareness about excessive sound exposure.

## 2. Ambulance Traffic Management System

**Tech Stack:** Computer Vision, Intelligent Traffic Management

A proposed system that detects an approaching ambulance and intelligently manages traffic signals to reduce waiting time and provide a faster route for emergency vehicles.
